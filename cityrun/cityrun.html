FAQ

Quick answers for players, press, and store reviewers.

Does City Run collect personal data?

No. City Run stores gameplay progress locally on your device (scores, settings, achievements). If you contact support, we use your message to respond.

Is the game offline?

Yes—core gameplay is available offline. Your progress saves locally.

What is “no-damage streak”?

It’s the distance you run while staying at full health. Take damage and the streak resets. Try to beat your best.
