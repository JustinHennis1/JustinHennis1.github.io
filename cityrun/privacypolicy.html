Privacy Policy

Game: City Run
Effective date: February 9, 2026

Short version: City Run does not collect or sell your personal information. The game stores gameplay progress (like scores and achievements) locally on your device. If you contact us, we only use your message to respond.

1) Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how City Run (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Game.

2) Information we collect

A. Information you provide

  • Support messages: If you email or otherwise contact us, you may provide information like your name, email address, device details, and the content of your message.

B. Information stored locally on your device

  • Gameplay data: The Game saves data such as high scores, achievement progress, unlocked characters/items, settings (e.g., button size/handedness), and similar gameplay state locally on your device.
  • No account required: You do not need to create an account to play.

C. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • We do not collect payment information (unless you make purchases through an app store, which is handled by the store).

3) Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

City Run does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs by default. If we add optional analytics, crash reporting, ads, or other third-party services in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide choices and disclosures.

4) How we use information

  • To provide core gameplay features (saving scores, settings, and achievement progress on your device).
  • To respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues you report.
  • To maintain and improve the Game.

5) How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • Legal reasons: if required to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
  • With your request/consent: if you ask us to share something (for example, sending debug logs).

6) Data retention

  • Local gameplay data: Stored on your device until you delete the Game or clear the app’s storage/data.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we may keep the message as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping.

7) Your choices and controls

  • You can reset or remove local game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.
  • You can choose not to contact us. If you do contact us, please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

8) Children’s privacy

City Run is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9) Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Local gameplay data is stored on your device and is protected by your device and operating system security features.

10) International users

If you contact us, your information may be processed in the country where we are located. By contacting us, you consent to this processing where permitted by law.

11) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above when changes are made.
